How it decides

  1. Reads

    Your real numbers — cash, tower HP, enemy scaling, and the live panel value and price of every upgrade. Not a default account, not a wiki table.

  2. Ranks

    A policy trained with PPO scores every upgrade by how much longer it expects that money to keep you alive. The answer changes as your account grows.

  3. Checks

    The numbers here come from running Wavecall and a deliberately dumb strategy on the same account, back to back, on the same day. When the gap is too small to measure, I say so instead of printing a number anyway.
